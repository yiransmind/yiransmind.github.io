<div class="programme-introduction">
<p>This programme examines how learners come to rely on AI as a source of knowledge and judgement. It considers how that reliance shapes the evaluation of evidence, independent judgement, and responsibility in educational settings.</p>
</div>

## Key questions

- How does assistance from AI become epistemic dependence?
- How can learners evaluate AI-generated claims and retain responsibility for their judgements?
- What educational practices support appropriate reliance on AI?
