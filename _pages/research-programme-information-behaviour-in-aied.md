<div class="programme-introduction">
<p>This programme examines information behaviour in artificial intelligence in education (AIED): how learners and educators seek, interpret, evaluate, and use AI-generated information. It connects questions of acceptance, engagement, dependence, and discontinuance with the ways people use AI in educational practice.</p>
</div>

## Key questions

- What shapes the acceptance and use of AI-generated information in learning?
- How do learners and educators evaluate and act on information provided by AI?
- When does AI use become dependence, and what leads people to discontinue its use?
