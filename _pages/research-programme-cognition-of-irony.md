<div class="programme-introduction">
<p>This programme examines the cognitive processes involved in understanding irony in communication. It focuses on how emojis, context, and cultural differences shape the interpretation and memory of ironic meanings.</p>
</div>

## Key questions

- How do emojis influence the comprehension and memory of irony?
- How do cultural contexts shape interpretations of irony and sarcasm?
- How do people combine verbal and contextual cues to infer a speaker’s meaning?
